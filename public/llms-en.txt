# Tier 0

> The operating layer for agent-run companies, with human sign-off on what matters.
> Tier 0 is an agent-native operating system for organizations and their lines of
> business: seven modules around one shared decision kernel (zeus). Agents carry
> execution, processes and daily operations; decisions are graded D0–D3 by importance
> and important ones wait for human sign-off; every record is Ed25519-sealed per-record
> and replayable. Commerce is the first line of business; common capability settles into
> a shared kernel, business-specific capability stays in applications, and the stack
> replicates to whatever comes next.

## Seven modules in three layers

Application layer (lines of business):
1. **Commerce OS** — the minimal commerce loop: product, content, gated listing, order
   pullback, refunds and customer service behind a pluggable ChannelAdapter; today an
   offline sandbox end to end; payment/fulfillment return 501.
2. **Loom** — the content base: a 13-stage content chain and candidate review; real model
   output fires only behind an explicit switch.
3. **Job Agent** — capability profiles for recruiting, consumer and business sides
   (existing; kernel wiring scheduled).

Platform layer (shared capabilities):
4. **Agent World** — the quality-gate pipeline: canvases, cost and quality metering,
   bidirectional MCP (existing; wired in M7).
5. **Atlas** — business-entity orchestration: NL-to-canvas, three approval channels,
   compliance, shadow canary and evolution gating (existing; wired in M7).

Kernel layer (the operating core):
6. **zeus** — the shared decision kernel: fan-out → deterministic aggregation → D0–D3
   grading → human approval → audit replay; HTTP API v1, idempotent decisions, an SLA
   timeout state machine, versioned policy; kernel v1 ready, durable storage in M6.
7. **Agent Dev** — the delivery pipeline: Blueprint → Preview → Production with
   acceptance evidence (existing; self-bootstrap in M5).

## Three operating principles

- **Agent-first**: agents carry execution, processes and daily operations; people are not
  in the execution loop.
- **Human sign-off**: decisions are graded D0–D3 with configurable thresholds; what counts
  as important enough for a person is set by the person.
- **Sealed and replayable**: every decision record is Ed25519-sealed per-record and
  replayable; tampering, deletion or reordering fails closed.

## Decision grades D0–D3

D0 auto; D1 async approval; D2 sync approval (money actions such as refunds); D3 blocked
(external actions such as publishing a listing). D2/D3 first return 423 DECISION_HELD and
execute only after human approval.

## Four engineering guarantees

- **Configurable grades**: thresholds are configurable; past decisions keep their
  policyVersion forever and are never rewritten.
- **Fail-closed by default**: an unregistered channel, a missing credential or an
  unreachable decision kernel is a refusal or 503, never a downgrade to local guessing.
- **Per-record seals**: each record is Ed25519-sealed as seq + prevHash + keyId + payload;
  verification recomputes the whole chain; deletion, reorder or forgery fails closed.
- **No auto commitment**: refund, compensation and promise intents are graded first; D2 and
  above always escalate to a human; a held decision never emits a customer-facing agent
  message.
